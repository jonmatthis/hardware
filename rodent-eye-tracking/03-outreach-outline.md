## Vendors (FRAMOS, ams-OSRAM, POC)

**Subject:** design-in inquiry, 3-camera head-mounted rodent eye tracker

1. Dear Blank, we're FreeMoCap, an open-source markerless mocap nonprofit.
2. We're building - Two eye cams + one world cam on a mouse head that weighs less than 4g, tethered,
   all electronics off-head, multiple rigs running at once.
3. We're asking for - Current eval path, cost per channel, fastest route to a live image on a PC.
4. Ask whether they do the design-in work or hand us to someone who does.
5. Note we own the CAD and capture software, so we're only buying sensor-to-USB.

Be brief on the initial contact and don't spec details like voltages. Best practice is to approach by asking for an outcome, the working from their response to the intended outcome.

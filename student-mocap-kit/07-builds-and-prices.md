## Tripod notes for US retail

**Could not find US retail cheap webcam with a 1/4-20 tripod thread.**

- The [C270](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C270-Desktop-Widescreen-Calling/dp/B004FHO5Y6) at $17.99 has **no tripod thread**. Nothing on the product page mentions one; the clip base just folds flat.
- The cheapest Logitech that does is the [C920x](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C920x-Pro-HD-Webcam/dp/B085TFF7M1) at **$59.99**. Logitech's own spec for the C920 family reads ["Tripod-ready universal mounting clip"](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/c920s-pro-hd-webcam.960-001257).
- The [C922x](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C922x-Pro-Stream-Webcam/dp/B01LXCDPPK) **includes a tripod in the box** but is **$110.04**.

**One possible fix is a clamp on the stand instead of just a screw.** A phone-holder clamp grips
anything, thread or no thread, and adds a couple of dollars in bulk. See the Stands section for
both halves: a light stand for height, plus a clamp where the camera has no thread.

**The practical answer on the mic is to spec it out, not shop around it.** At OEM level a
microphone is a line on the BOM the factory will happily delete, and it makes the unit cheaper. Any
RFQ should say "no microphone" explicitly. That keeps cheap consumer-style webcams (which come with
housings and clips) on the table instead of forcing us to bare modules.

## How to actually order, build, and sell

### Phase 1: prove one build (2 to 3 weeks, ~$400)
1. Buy **one** of every part at retail. Nothing bulk.
   [C270](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C270-Desktop-Widescreen-Calling/dp/B004FHO5Y6) ×3 **and**
   [C920x](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C920x-Pro-HD-Webcam/dp/B085TFF7M1) ×1 to compare,
   [NEEWER ST195 light stands](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DCV8NXYF) ×3, [ULANZI ST-06S clamps](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B1TRF224) ×3,
   [Monoprice #9470](https://www.monoprice.com/product?p_id=9470) ×3,
   one [Printastic](https://www.printastic.com/yard-signs/) board.
2. **Test whether the [InnoGear](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FKN716WF) phone holder grips a
   [C270](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C270-Desktop-Widescreen-Calling/dp/B004FHO5Y6) securely
   and holds an angle.** This is the gate on Build B, which is the build most likely to be the flagship.
3. Assemble a kit on the kitchen table. Time yourself. That number is your labour cost.
4. Run a full FreeMoCap capture. Check cable reach in a real room, camera placement, whether the
   Charuco detects cleanly, and whether
   [C920x](https://www.amazon.com/Logitech-C920x-Pro-HD-Webcam/dp/B085TFF7M1) autofocus can be locked.

### Phase 2: small real batch (4 to 6 weeks, ~$3,000)
1. Order **25 kits' worth** of whichever build won: cameras via
   [Amazon Business](https://business.amazon.com/) quantity discounts,
   75 [NEEWER ST195 light stands](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DCV8NXYF) and 75 [ULANZI clamps](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B1TRF224),
   75 [Monoprice #9470](https://www.monoprice.com/product?p_id=9470) at the 50+ tier,
   100 Charuco boards. Charuco at 100 rather than 25 because the price break is steep and they
   store flat.
2. Boards from a [Boston printer with pickup](https://www.banners-etc.com/corrugated-plastic-yard-signs)
   if the quote is competitive, otherwise [Printastic](https://www.printastic.com/yard-signs/).
3. Everything lands at the apartment. 25 kits is roughly a closet.
4. Assemble 5, list them, see if anyone buys.

### Phase 3: decide what you actually are
Only after selling those 25:
- Selling steadily → order [Alibaba](https://www.alibaba.com/trade/search?SearchText=1080p+usb+webcam+with+tripod) samples, work toward Build C economics.
- Selling slowly → stay on retail sourcing. Higher unit cost, zero inventory risk, and honestly fine.
- Not selling → you learned that for $3k instead of $30k.

### Selling on Shopify without the reliability problem
- **Hold real stock.** The unreliability you've hit is a drop-ship symptom. Stock in Boston means
  you control what ships.
- **One SKU to start.** Multiple tiers multiply support burden before you know which one sells.
- **Publish the exact model numbers** in the listing and the docs. Lets people buy the parts
  themselves, which fits the mission, and it's also free marketing for the assembled kit.
- **Set stock to a real number and let it hit zero.** Overselling is worse than being sold out.
- **Assume ~10% of units need replacing.** Buy spares in the batch, not after.

### Packing
Flat-pack around the Charuco board. It's 18"x24", rigid, and the largest item, so it's the natural
backing for everything else. A single box sized to the board with the cameras, tripods and cables
in a bag.

## Cameras, options only

FreeMoCap works with any UVC camera, so this is a price/quality choice, not a compatibility one.

| Camera | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Generic Shenzhen webcam | ~$8 to $14 est. | Clip + tripod usually included, which covers two bundle items at once |
| ELP board module | ~$20 to $30 est. | Board-level, M12 lens options |
| Arducam B0201 IMX291 | $55.99 | Color, 120° FOV, good low light |
| Arducam B0332 OV9281 | $49.99 | Global shutter, mono, 100fps, 70° H |
| Arducam B0322 OV2311 | $99.99 | Global shutter, mono, 2MP, 75° H |

Global shutter is sharper on fast motion. Both GS options are mono. It's a premium-tier question,
not a requirement.
